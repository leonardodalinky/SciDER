# NeurIPS 2026 Conference Guidelines

## Style File

Download `neurips_2026.sty` from https://media.neurips.cc/Conferences/NeurIPS2026/Formatting_Instructions_For_NeurIPS_2026.zip
and place it alongside `template.tex` in the template directory before running the workflow.

## Page Limit

- **9 pages** for the main text.
- Unlimited pages for references (not counted toward the limit).
- An optional appendix may follow the references and is not counted toward the limit,
  but reviewers are not required to read it.

## Mandatory Sections

- **Abstract** (≤ 250 words)
- **Introduction**
- **Related Work** (may be merged into Introduction or placed before Conclusion)
- **Method** (or "Approach", "Model", "Algorithm")
- **Experiments** (including setup, results, and ablations)
- **Conclusion**
- **References**
- **Broader Impact** (optional appendix section — discuss societal consequences)

## Formatting

- Single-column layout, 10pt font, US Letter paper size.
- Figures and tables must be numbered and captioned.
- NeurIPS style uses `natbib` with `abbrvnat` or `plainnat` citation style (`[Author, Year]`).
- Do not modify margins or font sizes from the official style file.

## Anonymity

- Submissions are **double-blind**: no author names, no affiliations, no acknowledgments.
- Self-citations must be phrased in third person (e.g., "Smith et al. [1] showed…").
- Do not link to non-anonymized code repositories in the submission.

## Supplementary Material

- Supplementary material (code, data, proofs) may be submitted separately.
- Reviewers may but are not required to consult it.
- The main paper must be self-contained.

## Literature Review Cutoff

- Do not cite papers published after the submission deadline.
- NeurIPS 2026 submission deadline: approximately May 2026 (check neurips.cc for exact date).
