# Simple Conference Guidelines (SciDER Default)

This is a minimal placeholder guideline used by SciDER when the caller does
not provide a real conference-guidelines file. It is venue-agnostic and
intentionally generic. If you are targeting a specific conference, replace
it by passing ``paper_conference_guidelines_path`` to the workflow, or drop
a real Call for Papers into ``inputs/conference_guidelines.md``.

## Page Limit

- **9 pages** for the main text, excluding references and appendix.
- References do not count toward the page limit.

## Mandatory Sections

Every submission must contain:
- **Abstract** (≤ 250 words)
- **Introduction**
- **Related Work**
- **Methodology** (or "Method", "Approach")
- **Experiments** (or "Evaluation", "Results")
- **Conclusion**
- **References**

## Formatting

- Single-column layout, 10pt font, US Letter paper size.
- Figures and tables must be numbered and captioned.
- Citations use the numeric style (e.g. `[1, 2]`).

## Submission Deadline

- Submission deadline: **2026-05-22 (anywhere on Earth)**.
- Literature review cutoff date: **2026-05-22** — do not cite any paper
  published after this date.

## Anonymity

- Submissions must be anonymous: no author names, no affiliations, no
  acknowledgments, no references to prior work in a way that reveals
  authorship.
- Self-citations are allowed but must be phrased in the third person.

## Supplementary Material

- An optional appendix may be submitted as an additional file. It is not
  counted toward the 9-page limit but reviewers are not required to read it.
